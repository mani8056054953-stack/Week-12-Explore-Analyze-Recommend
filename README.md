# Week-12-Explore-Analyze-Recommend

# Business Performance Analysis – Tableau Public

## Objective
The objective of this project is to explore business data, identify important patterns and trends, and provide meaningful business insights using Tableau Public.

## Visualizations
1. Sales by Business Category
2. Sales Trend Over Time
3. Sales vs Profit Analysis
4. Category and Region Performance Heat Map

## Interactive Filters
- Business Category
- Region
- Date/Year

## Key Findings
1. Business categories show different levels of sales performance.
2. Sales performance changes across different time periods.
3. Higher sales do not always result in higher profit.

## Suggestions
- Focus on high-performing business categories and regions.
- Analyze low-profit categories to improve profitability.

## Recommendations
The business should focus on high-profit categories and regions and regularly monitor sales and profit using interactive dashboards.

## Conclusion
The analysis shows that business performance varies across categories, regions, and time periods. The Tableau dashboard helps users explore these patterns interactively and supports better business decision-making.
